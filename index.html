Questions buyers ask

Is hosting really included?

Yes. Every website, store, app and portal plan includes managed hosting, SSL, daily backups and monitoring in the monthly fee.

Can I register my domain with you?

Yes. Search and add a domain at checkout. Registration is billed yearly; you remain the legal owner.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings or AI mentions?

No honest provider can. We guarantee the work: technical fixes, content and reporting, with visibility tracked every month.

How long does a website take?

Business websites typically launch in 2 to 4 weeks, corporate sites in 4 to 8, stores in 4 to 10, apps in 10 to 16.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once setup is paid, the design, content and domain are yours, documented in your agreement.
